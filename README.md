# 📊 Sales Analyzer

A simple yet powerful **Python-based sales data analysis tool** designed to process raw CSV transaction data, calculate key business metrics, identify sales trends, and generate actionable business insights.

The project analyzes thousands of sales transactions and provides insights into **revenue, orders, product performance, customers, categories, and daily sales trends**. It also supports date-range filtering and exporting category-specific datasets.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 💰 Revenue Analysis
- Calculates **total revenue** generated across all transactions.
- Calculates revenue generated by individual products.
- Breaks down revenue by product category.

### 📦 Order & Quantity Tracking
- Counts the **total number of orders**.
- Calculates the **total quantity of products sold**.
- Identifies the best-selling product based on total units sold.

### 👥 Customer Analysis
- Identifies the **top customer** based on total revenue generated.
- Helps understand customer contribution to overall sales.

### 📈 Sales Trends
- Generates **daily revenue trends**.
- Aggregates sales data based on transaction dates.
- Allows analysis of revenue patterns over time.

### 📅 Date-Range Filtering
- Calculate revenue between any custom **start and end date**.
- Useful for analyzing specific sales periods.

### 📁 Data Export
- Filters transactions based on product category.
- Exports filtered records into a separate CSV file.
- Example: Electronics → `electricals.csv`
